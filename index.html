Clinical Advantages

Why Early Heart Disease Screening Matters

Cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of global mortality. Early predictive screening allows for timely interventions, lifestyle modifications, and proactive clinical care.

Proactive Risk Assessment

Evaluates blood pressure, cholesterol, resting ECG, max heart rate, and ST slope to identify early signs of ischemia before severe symptoms occur.

Machine Learning Insight

Powered by Scikit-Learn Logistic Regression and DictVectorizer trained on clinical heart disease datasets for reliable probability scoring.

Cardiovascular Wellness

Essential Heart Health Guidelines

Heart-Healthy Nutrition

Prioritize whole grains, lean proteins, omega-3 fatty acids, and reduce saturated fats and refined sodium.

Regular Aerobic Activity

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise every week to maintain vascular elasticity.

Monitor Blood Pressure

Keep resting blood pressure below 120/80 mm Hg and conduct routine lipid panel screenings.

Stress & Sleep Control

Get 7-9 hours of restorative sleep and practice mindfulness to control cortisol and resting heart rate.
